# Caatuu expansion roadmap

Future directions to remember: more languages, the animated macaw adventure,
and custom voices. Recorded on 22 October 2026. These are ideas to revisit,
with no implementation scheduled by this note.

## More languages

Expand Caatuu with new language courses and learner-language pairs using the
shared application and language-pack system. Choose the next languages and
their scope when this work is taken up.

Start with the [language-pack guide](../tools/language-packs/README.md) and
[manual learning evaluation tools](../tools/learning-evaluation/README.md).
The [content expansion targets](COURSE_CONTENT_EXPANSION_TARGETS.md) separately
describe deeper content banks for courses.

## Animated macaw adventure

Continue the animation game built around the walking macaw and explorable
scenery when we are ready to resume it. The adventure remains a paused concept;
its walking animation, scenery studies and world-map direction are preserved.

The [resume guide](../apps/games/lab/RESUME.md) records the stopping point and
next decisions. The [walking source](../apps/games/lab/assets/macaw/walk/README.md)
holds the current walking and standing artwork.

## Custom voices with Gemini

Explore adding expressive custom voices to Caatuu using Gemini. The product
owner highlighted **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** and **Flash-Lite** on 22 October 2026
as especially impressive for voices and worth remembering for this expansion.

Cost estimate, 22 October 2026: for one saved voice, budget **$3–$5 per course
with Flash** or **$2–$4 with Flash-Lite**, allowing for instructions and some
retries. A fuller course is roughly 2–4 audio hours. All eight current courses
total roughly 17–25 hours: **$13–$20 Flash / $9–$13 Flash-Lite** in audio-output
charges before input charges and retries. Batch generation halves token costs.

These promotional prices apply through **31 December 2026**; listed rates
double on **1 January 2027**. Generate clips once and reuse them. See
[Google pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing#gemini-3.8-flash-tts).
Check language coverage, pronunciation and voice controls when resuming.
