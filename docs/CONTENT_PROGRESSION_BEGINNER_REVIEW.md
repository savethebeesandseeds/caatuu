# Content progression and beginner review

Date: 11 September 2026. Scope: all five authoritative Word World catalogs and
their coordinated listening/grammar starter material. This document records
the current 1–100 regrading and the earlier additions in the same initiative.

This is an AI editorial pass. It preserves licensing, pronunciation review,
native-speaker review and human-approval metadata. Structural validation does
not certify language quality; the modern-course phrases retain their existing
pending native-review status.

## Current authored controls

Every Word World record carries:

- `difficulty`: the retained course badge, 1–3.
- `usefulness`: practical communicative value, 1–100; higher is more useful.
- `complexity`: demand within the badge, 1–100; lower is easier.

These replace the old five-point names. Static usefulness is separate from a
learner's changing review urgency. A fixed number of completed encounters does
not establish familiarity: supported answers, independent recall and retention
over time belong to the learning profile. The catalog does not encode a
prerequisite graph or a mandatory repetition count.

The [catalog review](CONTENT_PROGRESSION_CATALOG_REVIEW.md) defines the anchored
scale, methodology, distributions and verification. Nearby scores represent
similar editorial judgments; they are not measured percentages. Source
numbers remain editable, and routine builds preserve them.
