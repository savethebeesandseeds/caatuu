Material corrections and limitations:

- New Mandarin listening cards retain the existing pinyin preview. Seventy-two draft word cards were deferred because no suitable authored guide was available; their valid Verb Nebula entries remain.
- 倒 was checked by sense: `dào` for pouring versus `dǎo` for falling over. A new pouring context supplies the exact pronunciation guide. This distinction is supported by the [Ministry of Education dictionary](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=1973&la=0&powerMode=0). No dictionary prose was copied into the content.
- Mandarin `打节拍` is authored as the complete lexical verb “keep the beat,” retaining its character readings. New character citations reference exact pronunciation units and respect the level of an available source sentence.
- One new Czech spoon context was deferred because the retained paradigm's `lžicí` alternative needs further verification. Existing `lžící`/`lžicí` records were preserved, not silently corrected.
- New Czech IDs are separate from modern shared concept IDs because Czech must rank the exact included English sentence, while modern courses use separately authored English retrieval descriptions. Existing shared IDs and English meanings were not changed.
- Qualified Czech, Mandarin, European Spanish and American English/Spanish review remains outstanding, especially lexical nuance, register, Czech variants and Mandarin neutral-tone/sandhi choices. Text checks cannot certify pronunciation quality or independent learner production.

The six existing Case curriculum objectives retain their transfer mechanism; six new contexts use its `transfer` phase. No learner assessment or held-out learner generalization study was performed. These games can check recognition and their existing matching/classification tasks; the added counts do not establish speaking, writing, or CEFR proficiency. The frozen quality rubric was not changed or rescored.
