## Credits to ship

Copy MUSIC_CREDITS.txt into the game's accessible Credits / Music screen, keeping the source and licence URLs. For a web game, make them clickable. Bundle this credits file and the corresponding licence texts with downloadable builds. The same attribution can be used for trailers that contain these recordings.

The MP3 audio has not been edited or transcoded; filenames were made easier to use. If the game later uses edited versions, identify the actual changes alongside the relevant credit, for example: "Edited for looping and faded for gameplay." Do not claim this modification unless it was actually made. Ordinary playback volume control need not be described as a newly edited recording.

CC BY 3.0 allows commercial use and adaptations. It does not contain a ShareAlike condition and does not require releasing your game's code or unrelated assets under a Creative Commons licence. Preserve the music's licence and copyright notices. Keep this music outside any blanket EULA prohibition that would restrict rights granted by its CC licence; do not apply DRM to these tracks in a way that prevents recipients exercising those rights. Credit must not imply that the artists endorse the game.

Sources: [CC BY 3.0 summary](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) and [full terms, especially sections 3 and 4](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode.en).

## Track-specific checks

- Salt Marsh Birds and Woodland Fantasy are published from Matthew Pablo's OpenGameArt account under CC BY 3.0. Both files also identify Matthew Pablo in their embedded artist metadata. The listings refer to an older attribution page at http://www.matthewpablo.com/services. Its HTTPS counterpart returned HTTP 404 during this check, so its historical wording could not be verified. The supplied credits use the available author, title, artist website, source and licence information required by standard CC BY attribution. His current homepage is https://www.matthewpablo.com/.
- Town Theme RPG explicitly lists CC0 and identifies cynicmusic as its author. The description also requests contact and mentions permission for other music on Pixelsphere. This particular OpenGameArt recording is expressly offered under CC0; its CC0 dedication does not require attribution, contact, or joining a mailing list. Both artist sites are included in the courtesy credit.
- Only the recordings attached to the selected pages were downloaded. These licence findings should not be assumed to cover other tracks on the artists' websites.

## Audio validation and limits

All three downloads are stereo MP3 files and decoded in full with FFmpeg without reported errors. Salt Marsh Birds and Woodland Fantasy are 48 kHz / 320 kbps; Town Theme RPG is 44.1 kHz / variable bitrate, approximately 108 kbps. They are the source MP3s; conversion to WAV would not restore missing audio detail. Seamless looping has not been established for these downloads.

The review establishes the permission published by the apparent creators, not an independent audit of every sample or a guarantee against automated platform claims. Keep this package's source records, original files, and hashes as evidence of what was obtained. There is no suggestion in the reviewed listings that these are Dofus recordings.
